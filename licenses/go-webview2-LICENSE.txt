F.E.A.R. Exclusive Console Content - third-party license: go-webview2

The installer's window shows its pages with Microsoft Edge WebView2 through go-webview2
(https://github.com/jchv/go-webview2, commit 56598839c808a2340edee99204db479f410e9bf4),
which is compiled into FEAR-Exclusive-Console-Content-Installer.exe in a modified form; the
changes are published with the mod's source code. It includes portions of webview by Serge
Zaitsev (https://github.com/webview/webview), covered by the same license.
