F.E.A.R. Exclusive Console Content - third-party license: go-winloader

FEAR-Exclusive-Console-Content-Installer.exe loads the WebView2 loader from inside itself
with go-winloader (https://github.com/jchv/go-winloader, commit
c1995be93bd1362cba4d3d0ba6bfe0efdd0bcaf8), compiled in with a one-line change that is
published with the mod's source code. Its license is the ISC license below.
